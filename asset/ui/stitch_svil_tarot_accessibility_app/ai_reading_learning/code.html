Archetypal Frameworks

The Fool represents new beginnings, having faith in the future, being inexperienced, not knowing what to expect, having beginner's luck, improvisation and believing in the universe.

When this card appears in a reading, it suggests you are at the precipice of a significant leap. The clinical interpretation advises observing risk tolerance—are you stepping into the void prepared, or purely on impulse?
